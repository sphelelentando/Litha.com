AMBASSADOR PROGRAMME

Represent the ascent.

For creators, athletes, professionals and culture-shapers who naturally live the values behind L.I.T.H.A.

What we look for

We value consistency, professionalism, authentic style and the ability to create clean, high-quality content. Audience size matters less than alignment, credibility and the way you represent yourself.

What ambassadors can receive

Depending on campaign and performance: complimentary products, fragrances, personal discount codes, sales commission, campaign opportunities, event invitations and potential progression into deeper creative partnerships.

Core expectations

  • Represent L.I.T.H.A. professionally online and offline.
  • Create authentic product and lifestyle content.
  • Tag the official brand account and use assigned campaign information.
  • Give useful feedback on products, sizing, packaging and customer experience.
  • Respect confidentiality around unreleased products and campaigns.
